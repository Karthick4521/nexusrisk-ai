ESCALATION POLICY

Review Priority Categories
  LOW (score 0-24): No significant risk signals identified. No escalation
  recommended based on the supplied transaction history alone.

  MEDIUM (score 25-54): One or more risk signals identified. Recommended
  for standard-queue investigator review during normal case processing.

  HIGH (score 55-89): Multiple risk signals identified, potentially
  including large transactions, bursts, or behavior deviation. Recommended
  for prioritized investigator review.

  HIGH PRIORITY HUMAN REVIEW (score 90+): A significant cluster of risk
  signals has been identified across multiple rules, often including
  related transaction patterns (R06). This case should be escalated to a
  human investigator promptly. This category is a triage priority signal
  only - it is not a finding of fraud.

Escalation Conditions
  - Any case reaching HIGH or HIGH_PRIORITY_HUMAN_REVIEW must clearly
    display the Human Review Required notice.
  - If Gemini or the local policy retrieval component fails for any
    reason, deterministic findings must still be displayed in full;
    escalation categorization does not depend on the AI layer.
  - Missing or insufficient information must be explicitly disclosed to
    the investigator rather than silently ignored or assumed.

AI Limitations and Safety
  - The system must never state that fraud has occurred.
  - The system must never fabricate evidence, transactions, or
    beneficiaries not present in the supplied data.
  - Uncertainty must always be stated explicitly when information is
    insufficient to reach a confident interpretation.
